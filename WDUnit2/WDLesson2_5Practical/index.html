Bosses

The bosses of Hollow Knight are mysterious creatures that live in the underground kingdom of Hallownest, each with their own unique appearance, abilities, and fighting styles. Some are enormous beasts, while others are skilled warriors who challenge the Knight with fast and unpredictable attacks. Every boss has its own personality and backstory, making each feel different and exciting. From terrifying monsters to ancient guardians and infected warriors, the bosses test the player's skill, patience, and determination. Defeating them allows the Knight to explore deeper parts of Hallownest and uncover more secrets about the kingdom's dark past.
